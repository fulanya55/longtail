# Heterogeneous parameter-routing route closure audit

## Boundary

This is a read-only synthesis of completed fresh-identity development studies.  It does not rerun a model, read a consumed independent/locked test, choose a checkpoint, alter a threshold, or create an additional data identity.  Every source experiment below records `test_generated=false`, `test_opened=false`, and `locked_test_used=false`.

The audit separates a useful empirical baseline fact from a mechanism claim.  An improvement over an unmatched legacy global head is not evidence that the proposed routing inductive bias, rather than branch capacity or another shared architectural change, caused the improvement.

## Consequences

1. The frozen P1n MLCNN plus IQ-grouped route may remain a **reference-only empirical baseline**.  It may not be advertised as a confirmed heterogeneous-routing innovation, nor as a general all-12 parameter solution.
2. P7a closes the direct IQ-grouping claim.  P6a-r1 and P6b close the available additions of fixed raw-I/Q physics plus detached posterior FiLM, and final-token class-specific local querying, respectively.
3. None of these stops turns a small positive C1-vs-C0 change into a candidate for five-seed confirmation.  The required matched-control effect was absent before confirmation identity generation.

## Excluded retries

Do not retune, rerun, or select among the following on their consumed pools: P7a generic-control width/channel/kernel choices; P6a FFT/ACF descriptor, FiLM coefficient, feature count or loss; P6b query dimension, temperature, adapter, global control or loss.  Do not reinterpret the earlier P4c/P4d confirmation pools as a replacement for P7a's matched control.

## Gate for a future routing candidate

A new routing proposal must differ in its information source and its causal comparison, not merely in head width or a variant of the closed conditioning/query choices.  Before any new development training it needs a preregistration with:

1. a fixed new observable or structural hypothesis and an equal-MAC/equal-parameter non-routing control that retains all generic capacity;
2. fresh train/development identities, zero-overlap audit, identical classification/parameter optimization budget and checkpoint rule;
3. full-12 oracle and predicted-gate outcomes, per-target systemic-harm checks, macro-AUPR/subset safety, and an explicit cost contract;
4. promotion only after the three-seed matched-control effect passes.  Five-seed statistics and fresh confirmation then remain mandatory before any new locked test is proposed.
