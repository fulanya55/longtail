## Non-negotiable failure interpretations

1. P8b is a response-amplification failure: all three 10 dB VFT AUPR deltas are negative and worst-power VFT FPR increases, so it cannot be described as recall recovery or long-tail success.
2. P8c is a difficult-negative separation failure: VFT 10 dB AUPR decreases in two of three seeds, hard-negative FPR increases in two, worst-power VFT FPR increases in all three, and separation decreases in all three.  Its `cap=0` does not control the calibration decision point (`p=0.05`), but replacing that cap, adding OHEM/ranking, or tuning thresholds is a prohibited rescue rather than evidence for a new mechanism.
3. P2u/P2v establish an oracle boundary for the current VFT Doppler observable family: even label-oracle selection over the frozen positive grid, phase increment and constant fallback is insufficient on near-low / near-high OOD.  Do not build a deeper VFT router, use the same Doppler/phase features, or attach a new VFT mechanism without an independent observable.
4. P3o-O is an oracle-positive, equal-parameter test of adjacent-peak relations.  Its three seeds fail the MAE/Pearson/ultra-close gates; deeper count-expert search is prohibited on the same mechanism family.
5. P5e activation coverage is not lazy-execution acceleration.  Its dense JNR attachment is valid bounded parameter evidence, but no current artifact proves output-equivalent end-to-end acceleration.
6. P3o-S is a causal oracle diagnosis, not a classifier improvement: exact MFT subtraction reduces MFT-induced ISRJ FPR but sacrifices MFT AP and macro-AUPR.  P3o-R and P3o-L respectively fail deployable support recovery and material posterior-conditioned parameter improvement.  No MFT residual/router may be built without a new independent physical observation and its own sufficiency screen; see `docs/p3o_isrj_mft_route_closure_audit_20260803.md`.
7. P7a is the equal-MAC/equal-parameter control required to attribute the IQ-grouped route.  It fails its C1-vs-C2 effect, Pearson and SawFM target-safety gates.  P6a-r1 physical FiLM and P6b temporal-query pooling separately fail full-12 or equal-capacity-control gates.  Do not use P4c/P4d's C1-vs-C0 gains as proof of a routing mechanism, and do not tune the closed controls on their consumed identity pools; see `docs/heterogeneous_parameter_routing_route_closure_audit_20260803.md`.

## Required evidence before a final candidate may be frozen

1. A single candidate and necessary ablations must pass an equal-compute five-model study with paired statistics and two new identity confirmations.
2. Every claimed target must report predicted-gate full-12 effects, worst combination, per-power FPR/recall and difficult-negative separation; no macro average may conceal a negative target/cell.
3. Generalization claims must separately report parameter OOD, composition-disjoint and the four-axis long-tail protocol.  A failure on one axis remains in the final table.
4. A computational claim requires a fresh, output-equivalent, CUDA-isolated benchmark.  The P5 environment gate must be met before any new cost identities are created.
5. Only after items 1--4 are frozen may a new locked-test preregistration be submitted for explicit user approval.  No existing locked or consumed independent test may be reread, retuned or reused.

## Current decision

The project has credible **bounded Level-3 joint-perception evidence**, not a completed long-goal candidate.  The next research action must be either an independent-observable, preregistered mechanism (not a VFT Doppler/phase, ranking, cap or threshold variant) on a new train-only identity pool, or an authorized isolated-GPU execution for the computation branch.  Existing nine-power views do not constitute that independent observation because they share component and receiver-noise substreams; see `docs/independent_observation_contract_audit_20260803.md`.  It must not be a relaxation, rerun or depth expansion of a stopped route.
