# A-final-prelock decision

The anti-aliased F2 downsampling candidate is frozen at the development/prelock boundary after two independent five-seed screens. Across the ten paired seed/split observations, the mean 30 dB exact-combination gain is +0.6836 percentage points, with unchanged parameter count and no locked-test access.

This is not a claim of universal power robustness or strict single-split p<0.05 significance. The known limitations are a small 20 dB AUPR trade-off, variable 35--45 dB effects, and a borderline paired test within each split. The manifest points to immutable source checkpoint hashes and must not be retuned.

Any locked-test action requires explicit user authorization. Before authorization, no test artifact may be opened and no model/threshold adjustment is permitted.
