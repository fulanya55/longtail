# Route A H3: frozen unified-candidate external confirmation

Date: 2026-08-03  
Status: preregistered before H3 source generation.

## Question

The existing candidate `H1_B2_MFTSharedDecision_VFTRidgeFusion_v2` has passed
construction and a line-held-out v12 confirmation, but v12 was previously
consumed by another research line. H3 asks one falsifiable question:

> With all five seed-specific candidate artifacts, feature normalizers,
> physical experts, calibrators, thresholds and the MFT shared-decision policy
> frozen, does the complete six-label candidate retain its preregistered
> improvement over H1 on a newly generated, identity-isolated standard
> six-label source?

H3 is a pure external confirmation. It changes no model, loss, feature,
calibrator, threshold, fusion coefficient or source parameter after this
document is written.

## Frozen candidate and controls

- Candidate: `H1_B2_MFTSharedDecision_VFTRidgeFusion_v2`, manifest
  `frozen/20260801_route_a_h1_b2_mft_vft_shared_policy_v2/frozen_manifest.json`.
- Baseline Q0: frozen H1 with the same frozen RawSaw SawFM replacement.
- Mechanism controls: frozen Q3 VFT base-only refit head and Q5 frozen
  shuffled-Ridge head are evaluated, but never fitted on H3.
- Seeds: 2039--2043; every referenced artifact SHA-256 must match the frozen
  manifest before inference.
- The evaluator may reconstruct frozen fit-role normalization statistics only
  to verify their saved hashes; it must not fit a new statistic on H3.

## New source and roles

- Train-only external source: 25,200 standard six-label records, all 63
  nonempty combinations x 400, generated by the official balanced generator.
- Base sample seed: 134,000,000; range 134,000,001--134,025,200.
- A pre-generation receiver identity audit must show zero overlap with every
  readable historic train source. No test split is generated.
- H3 labels and physical parameters are evaluation-only. No fitting,
  calibration, threshold search, early stopping or architecture selection is
  permitted on H3.

## Metrics and gates

All metrics use the frozen calibration/thresholds. The unified candidate Q6
must, relative to Q0, simultaneously satisfy:

1. macro-AUPR >= +0.005 and macro-F1 >= +0.010;
2. MFT/VFT/ISRJ mean weak recall >= +0.030 and mean weak-vs-negative AUROC
   >= +0.015;
3. every target AUPR delta >= -0.010; target mean negative FPR <= 0.20;
4. strong-positive target recall does not decline by more than 0.010;
5. at least 4/5 seeds have positive target weak-recall delta;
6. RawSaw SawFM probabilities and decisions are exactly unchanged;
7. MFT has zero Q1-negative to Q6-positive flips and its high-order hard FPR
   is reduced by at least 0.010 on average;
8. the frozen aligned VFT route exceeds both frozen Q3 and Q5 weak-AUROC by
   at least 0.005, with at least 4/5 positive seeds; and
9. source, frozen-artifact and test-v3-seal contracts pass.

Paired five-seed bootstrap CIs are reported as descriptive confirmation
statistics. A fail closes H3 with no post-H3 repair; passing H3 only advances
the evidence ledger and does not authorize test-v3, which still requires all
remaining ablation, literature-baseline and robustness conditions.

## Prohibitions

No historical locked test, test-v2, test-v3 I/Q/labels, calibration role,
threshold role or H3-label-dependent model fitting may be used. H3 cannot be
used to select a different model if it fails.
