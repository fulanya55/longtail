# Route A H1u stop decision

## Closed claim

The claim that a fixed correct-LFM three-band residual produces a stable
learned weak-MFT advantage in the H1u separated-delay six-label source is
rejected by its pre-registered five-seed development evidence.

## Why the result is informative

H0u had already separated the physical question from learning: the correct
fixed statistic is observable and exceeds the phase control in a zero-fit
paired screen. H1u therefore rules out the stronger, practically relevant
claim that this particular three-number residual is a sufficient learned
classifier improvement. It does not refute physical observability in general.

## Prohibited follow-ons

The following would be an unregistered rescue of H1u and are prohibited:

- selecting delay bands, JNR, replica geometry, source seed or source size
  from H1u results;
- normalizing, widening, gating, relocating or otherwise re-tuning this
  residual in H1u;
- changing its loss, backbone, epoch budget, optimizer, calibration or
  threshold;
- evaluating a repaired H1u arm on any historical locked test or test-v3.

## What a valid next study must be

It must test a new mechanism with a fresh, preregistered source and a new
identity-isolated data role. Before any candidate training, the protocol must
show both (1) a genuine weak-component ranking gap in a frozen fair baseline
and (2) an information mechanism not equivalent to the closed three-number
MFT residual. A fresh study is not allowed to reinterpret H1u's early role as
a validation source.
