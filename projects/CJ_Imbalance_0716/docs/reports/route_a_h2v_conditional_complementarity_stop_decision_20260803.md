# Route A H2v stop decision

H2v's eligibility gate failed because one of five independently split,
independently initialized B0 runs had only 4.07% weak-vs-absent inversion
pairs, below the preregistered 5% minimum. The candidate correction was not
trained.

The prohibition is specific and binding: do not change the H2v ladder,
source, source size, identity split, baseline, error-set cutoff, LFM statistic,
gate, calibration, threshold or model budget and then rerun H2v. Do not use
its early-role results for model selection.

The appropriate next branch is a newly preregistered unified six-label
development study, not another MFT-only residual or admission-source rescue.
It must directly compare capacity-matched C0--C4-style baselines and isolate
the visibility-balanced loss/gate mechanism under fresh receiver identities.
